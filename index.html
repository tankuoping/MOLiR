Why three chapters?

Read your life like tree rings

We remember different things from different stages. Recent years bring up achievements and experiences. Going further back uncovers the values, lessons and influences that made us who we are.

Recent Years · last 1–3 years

Achievements, contributions, what fulfils you now

Growth Years · 4–10 years ago

Transitions, new skills, challenges that grew you

Foundations · 10+ years ago

Formative experiences, core values, recurring themes

Grounded in Career Construction Theory (Savickas), Life Review Theory (Butler) and Narrative Identity Theory (McAdams).

The y-axis

Plot your energy, not just events

⚡ Why energy?

Energy fuels how we show up every day. Some activities fill us with energy and joy; others leave us tired or drained.

📈 Why map the highs and lows?

Over time, the pattern shows which activities, habits and situations sustain you and which deplete you. That gives you clues for designing a more balanced life.

Adapted from Burnett & Evans, Designing Your Life (2016).

Step 1 · Map your journey

Questions to jog your memory

Foundations

10+ years ago
  • Which experiences most shaped who you are today?
  • Which values have guided your decisions all along?
  • What themes keep showing up across your journey?

Growth Years

4–10 years ago
  • What big transitions or changes did you navigate?
  • What new skills, perspectives or capabilities did you build?
  • Which challenges helped you grow the most?

Recent Years

last 1–3 years
  • Which achievement or contribution are you proudest of?
  • Which activities or projects felt most fulfilling?
  • What experiences do you want more of in future?
